# Speek privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

Speek is a macOS voice assistant that runs on your Mac. It is an open-source personal app; there is no Speek server and no Speek account.

## What Speek accesses

- **Your voice and text.** Recordings and typed requests are sent to the speech and AI providers you choose in Speek (for example OpenAI or OpenRouter, or Codex on your Mac) to transcribe and answer them.
- **Google data (Gmail and Google Calendar).** Only when you connect them and ask for something, Speek reads or changes the specific emails, drafts, or calendar events needed for that request, through Google's official MCP servers. That content is sent to your chosen AI provider to complete the request.
- **Other apps and services you connect** (such as Notion, Linear, GitHub, or Apple apps) are accessed the same way: only for a request you make.

## What Speek stores

Everything Speek keeps stays on your Mac: settings, chat history, memory, dictation history, and sign-in tokens (in an owner-only file or the macOS Keychain). You can delete it by removing Speek's data in `~/Library/Application Support/Speek` and `~/Library/Application Support/com.aveekpatra.speek`.

## What Speek does not do

Speek does not sell, share, or transfer your data to anyone other than the providers you choose for your own requests. It does not use your data for advertising and does not collect analytics.

## Google API Services

Speek's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
